Who it is for

Different backgrounds. One bridge.

You do not need to already be a bioinformatician, programmer or AI specialist. The goal is awareness: seeing where your current knowledge can lead when combined with technology.

01 · Example pathway

Life Sciences

Explore how biological knowledge can connect with bioinformatics, genomics, data analysis and computational research.

02 · Example pathway

Linguistics

Discover how language knowledge can connect with natural language processing, language technology, AI and computational approaches to language.

03 · Example pathway

Technology & Computing

See how technical skills can support research, products, automation, data-driven work and collaboration across disciplines.

Two-day programme

Awareness first. Action next.

The programme is designed to move from understanding the bridge to identifying practical next steps.

Day 01

Understanding the Bridge

Start with the big picture: what technology can look like from different academic and professional backgrounds.

  • What is bioinformatics and why does it matter?
  • Biology, data and computation: where they meet
  • Linguistics, language data and AI
  • Examples of interdisciplinary pathways
  • Questions, discussion and awareness gaps
Day 02

From Awareness to Action

Turn exposure into direction by looking at skills, projects and realistic ways to begin.

  • Choosing a pathway that fits your background
  • Skills and tools to learn first
  • How projects can become evidence of capability
  • Research, career and learning pathways
  • Next steps with HiScript Academy
