About HiScript

Technology belongs in every discipline.

HiScript is a technology brand building useful solutions and creating pathways for people to build with technology — whatever their academic, professional or creative starting point.

Explore what we build →Explore the Academy
The idea

Different disciplines. Shared possibilities.

Technology is not a discipline that has to sit apart from everything else. It can become a layer that helps people research, communicate, teach, create, analyse, automate, organise and solve problems.

That is the space HiScript is building in: connecting technology with the knowledge, questions and real-world problems that already exist across society.

Technology × Every Discipline

There is no single doorway into technology.

Biology, business, education, language, engineering, the arts, social sciences and computing can all become starting points for technology work.

Science & ResearchData, computation, modelling, analysis and research tools.
Business & SocietyDigital systems, automation, decision support and better workflows.
Education & LearningLearning technology, data-informed education and accessible tools.
Arts & HumanitiesCreative technology, digital expression, language and cultural work.
Health & LifeBioinformatics, health data, research computing and digital systems.
Engineering & TechnologySoftware, AI, infrastructure, systems and technical innovation.
